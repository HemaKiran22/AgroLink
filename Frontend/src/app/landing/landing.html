What AgroLink Offers

Everything in one agricultural platform

AgroLink connects the core activities of farmers and agricultural sellers in one application.

🌾

Crop Management

Farmers can add, view, update and manage crop information.

🧴

Agrochemicals

Farmers can discover available agrochemicals while sellers manage their product listings.

Product Requests

Farmers can request products and sellers can review and update request status.

Feedback

Registered users can share ratings and feedback through the platform.
